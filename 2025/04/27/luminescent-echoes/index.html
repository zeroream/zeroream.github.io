Poem

Luminescent Echoes

by Rhosemarie Gianan

Fiddling crystal balls,

As the lights continue to flicker to different colors.

Laughter encompasses the gallery.

Sweetness surrounds the streets—

Something wet rolls down on my cheeks,

As I put back the images of worn-out hourglass—

Darkness surrounds once full of warm wraiths.
